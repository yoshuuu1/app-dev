# My Favorite Movie

## Spider-Man: No Way Home

**Genre:** Action, Adventure, Science Fiction

*One of the most exciting superhero movies I have watched.*

### About the Movie

Spider-Man: No Way Home is about Peter Parker, whose identity as Spider-Man becomes public. He faces many challenges while trying to protect the people he loves.

### Why I Like It

I like this movie because:
- The story is exciting.
- The action scenes are amazing.
- It teaches the importance of friendship and responsibility.

### My Favorite Quote

> With great power comes great responsibility.

### My Favorite Character

**Spider-Man (Peter Parker)**

He is my favorite because he is brave, helpful, and always tries to do what is right.

### Movie Rating

 **5/5**
